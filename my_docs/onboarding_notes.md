# Northwind Cold Storage - onboarding notes (fictional sample)

New warehouse staff complete two days of induction before working alone.
Day one covers hygiene rules and the handheld scanner. Day two covers the
loading docks and the forklift safety briefing.

Shifts run from 06:00 to 14:00 and from 14:00 to 22:00. The night shift was
discontinued in March 2025.

Lost access cards are replaced by the front desk for a fee of 15 euros.
The staff canteen is on the second floor and closes at 15:30.
